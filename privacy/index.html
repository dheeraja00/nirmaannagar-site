Nirmaan Nagar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you play Nirmaan Nagar on iPhone, iPad or Android.

Who we are

Nirmaan Nagar is made and run by Dheeraj Agrawal in India ("we", "us"). We decide what the game collects and why.

Questions about your data, requests and complaints: write to support@nirmaannagar.com. Dheeraj Agrawal reads and answers every message.

The short version

  • You can play without sending us anything. Choose "Only on this phone", or "Under 18", and nothing about you or your town is sent.
  • If you are 18 or older, you can keep your town online. Then a random player ID and your towns are kept with Unity, our game service.
  • Crash reports, play statistics and online Missions are each a separate yes. You can say no and still play.
  • No ads. No advertising ID. We do not sell your data or use it to track you. There is no chat, and you never type your name.
  • You can delete it all in the game: Settings → Online → Delete my account. Our deletion page has every way: https://nirmaannagar.com/delete.

When you first open the game

The game asks how old you are: "Under 18" or "18 or older". Your answer stays on your phone. We use it only to keep players under 18 offline.

If you are 18 or older, the game then asks: "Keep your town online?"

  • Only on this phone: nothing about you or your town is sent. One exception: if this phone was online before and something is still waiting to be deleted (a deletion you asked for, or old play statistics), the game connects only to finish deleting it.
  • Keep my town online: see "What is kept online" below.

Nothing goes online before you answer. The questions about crash reports and play statistics come right after "Keep my town online". The question about online Missions comes the first time you tap "Play online".

What stays only on your phone

For every player:

  • your towns, their names and the spare copies the game keeps in case a save goes wrong;
  • your settings, and your answers to the game's questions;
  • your Missions nickname choice, and the players you muted or avoid;
  • photo mode's album: your last 24 photos, and the first photo of a new town. They stay in the game's own storage on your phone, out of your iCloud or Google backup. They are deleted when you use "Delete my account" or delete the game;
  • reminders, if you turn them on. Your phone makes them; no server sends them.

Your photos leave your phone only if you share them, or save them to your photo library yourself. To save one on iPhone or iPad, the game asks iOS for permission to add photos; on Android it adds the photo to Pictures/Nirmaan Nagar, which needs no permission. Either way, it cannot see the photos already in your library.

A small key. If you chose online, the game keeps a key in your phone's secure storage. If you said yes to play statistics, their random ID is kept there too.

  • iPhone and iPad: the key is in the iOS keychain, so your town can come back if you delete and reinstall the game. It stays on this phone even after you delete the game, and is deleted when you delete your online data in the game.
  • Android: the key is in the game's own storage, locked with a key from Android's Keystore that never leaves the phone. It is deleted when you delete your online data in the game, or delete the game. A reinstalled game then cannot find your online town by itself, unless Android brings back the game's data from your Google backup.

Your phone's backup. iOS and Android may back up the game's files and settings (your towns and your answers) to your iCloud or Google account, under Apple's or Google's terms, if you have backup turned on. The photo album and the Android key are left out.

Three extra choices, each its own yes

These are asked only after you choose online. You can change each one at any time in Settings → Online.

Crash reports (Sentry)

  • What: when the game crashes or hits an error, a report of:
    • what went wrong;
    • the game's last few steps (such as "placed a prop") and the technical messages it wrote just before;
    • the game's version;
    • your phone's model and system, memory, screen, language and time zone.
  • Not included: no name, no player ID and nothing about your town. Every report carries the same word, "player", so reports cannot be told apart by person.
  • Your internet address: Sentry sees it when a report arrives. We have set Sentry not to store it, and to drop any location.
  • Why: to find and fix crashes.
  • Off: nothing more is sent, and reports not yet sent are deleted from your phone.

Play statistics (Unity Analytics)

  • What: how you play:
    • sessions, guide steps, levels, coins earned and spent, props and land bought, tasks, missions, which photo formats you share or save (never the photos), settings used, and how smoothly the game runs;
    • the game's version, and your phone's model and system, processor and graphics, memory, screen, and language and region setting.
  • The ID: a random ID kept for statistics alone, not your player ID. Unity may tell a rough country from your connection.
  • Why: to make the game better.
  • Off: nothing more is sent, and Unity is asked to delete what it holds. Unity says it does this within 7 days.

Play Missions online (Unity Lobby and Relay)

  • When: asked the first time you tap "Play online".
  • What goes to Unity while you play:
    • your internet address and player ID;
    • the room, its code and the round;
    • your nickname. You pick it from the game's list; you never type it.
  • Other players: they see your nickname and what you do in the round. Their phones also get your player ID, so the round knows who is who and they can mute or avoid you. They never see your internet address or your town.
  • How long: Unity keeps connection records up to 30 days.
  • Off: you play Missions with bots instead.

What we never collect

Your name, email, phone number or home address. Your contacts, the photos in your library, or your exact location. Your voice. An advertising ID. Messages: there is no chat, only the game's fixed calls. Payments: there are no purchases.

Who handles your data for us

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services) handles:
    • player IDs and sign-in links (Authentication);
    • towns (Cloud Save);
    • online Missions (Lobby and Relay);
    • play statistics (Analytics);
    • a small server task (Cloud Code) that ends your Sign in with Apple link when you delete your account.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) keeps crash reports.
  • Apple runs Sign in with Apple and Game Center, under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Google runs Android and Google Play. If you install the game from Google Play, Google handles the download. If you let your phone share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google also shows us crash and performance figures for the game (Android vitals), which carry no player ID. Google does this under its own privacy policy.

Unity and Sentry handle your data only for us, under their terms with us, which require them to protect it at least as well as this policy says. We share your data with no one else, unless the law makes us. Their own policies: Unity, Sentry, Apple, Google.

Where your data is kept

Outside India:

  • crash reports in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany);
  • online Missions rounds through Unity's server in Mumbai, India;
  • the rest with Unity, which does not say exactly where. It may be the United States or another country.

India's law allows this unless the government restricts a country.

Players under 18

If you choose "Under 18":

  • the game stays on your phone;
  • there is no cloud save, no account, no crash reports, no statistics and no online Missions (you play with bots);
  • nothing is sent.

We do not ask for proof of age. If you are under 18, please choose "Under 18".

Parents: if your child chose "18 or older" by mistake, delete the online data in the game (Settings → Online → Delete my account), or write to us with the player ID and we will delete it.

Your rights and choices

  • Stop keeping your town online: Settings → Online → Keep it on this phone only. This deletes the online copy and your player ID. Your town stays on the phone. It is as easy as saying yes was.
  • Turn crash reports, play statistics or online Missions off: the switches in Settings → Online.
  • Delete everything, online and on this phone: Settings → Online → Delete my account.
  • Ask what we hold, correct it, get a copy of your town, or delete it: write to us with your player ID.
  • Name someone to use these rights for you if you cannot.

We hold no name or email for you, so we find your data by your player ID. Play statistics use a separate random ID that we cannot find from your player ID: to delete them, turn statistics off in the game, or delete your account in the game.

Complaints

  1. Write to us first: support@nirmaannagar.com. We reply within 7 days and settle your request or complaint within 30 days.
  2. About your data: if we do not sort it out, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  3. About the game as an online game: you can appeal to the Online Gaming Authority, set up under India's Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025, within 30 days of our answer, or of the day our answer was due.

If you live where other privacy laws apply, such as the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have similar rights there, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your data

The steps are on our deletion page: https://nirmaannagar.com/delete.

Keeping your data safe

  • Everything the game sends travels encrypted.
  • Your phone's key is kept in the iOS keychain, or on Android in the game's own storage locked with Android's Keystore. Only this game can read it.
  • Only the people who run the game can reach the services' dashboards.

If a data breach affects you, we will tell you in the game and on this site without delay, and tell the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If we start collecting something new, or using it for something new, the game asks you again before anything new is sent.
