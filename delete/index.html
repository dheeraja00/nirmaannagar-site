Delete your Nirmaan Nagar data

Last updated: 25 October 2026

If you chose "Only on this phone", or "Under 18", nothing about you or your town was sent to us. There is nothing online to delete. Deleting the game from your phone deletes your town and your photo album.

If you chose "Keep my town online", you can delete what is kept online at any time.

In the game (the fastest way)

  1. Make sure your phone is connected to the internet.
  2. Tap Settings, then the Online tab.
  3. Choose one:
ButtonWhat it deletesThen
Keep it on this phone only (confirm with Delete online copy)Your towns saved online, old towns put aside, and your player IDYour town stays on this phone. Nothing more is sent.
Delete my account (confirm with Delete everything)All of the above, plus your towns and your photo album on this phoneA new town begins.

After "Delete my account": your choice to play online stays on, so the new town goes online with a brand-new player ID. To keep nothing online, tap Keep it on this phone only as well.

Not connected? The Online tab says "Connect to the internet to change or delete what is kept online."

Lost the connection halfway? The deletion finishes by itself the next time you are online. Nothing goes back up meanwhile.

What deleting also does

  • Crash reports: stop at once. Reports not yet sent are deleted from your phone.
  • Play statistics: stop at once. Unity is asked to delete them, and says it does so within 7 days.
  • Sign in with Apple: if you kept your town with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm, and the game ends the link with Apple.
  • Game Center link: deleted with your player ID.
  • The key in your phone's keychain: deleted.

A few things stay for a short time

  • Online Missions: Unity keeps connection records (your internet address and player ID) for up to 30 days, then deletes them.
  • Crash reports already sent: they stay in Sentry for up to 30 days. They carry no player ID, so we cannot pick out yours.
  • Records the law requires, for as long as it requires.

If you have deleted the game

Deleting the game does not delete your online town.

Way 1: install it again and delete in the game

  1. Install the game again. Your town can come back:
    • on the same phone, from the key in its keychain;
    • on any phone signed in to the same Game Center account;
    • on any iPhone, if you kept your town with Apple: first tap Settings → Online → Log in with Apple.
  2. Answer 18 or older, then Keep my town online.
  3. If the game asks "Welcome back!", choose either answer.
  4. Tap Settings → Online → Delete my account.

Way 2: write to us

Write to support@nirmaannagar.com with:

  • the subject "Delete my data";
  • your player ID;
  • your town's level, or roughly when you last played, so we know the town is yours.

You find your player ID in Settings → Your town or Settings → Online ("Player ID ..."), while the game is connected.

We hold no name or email for you, so your player ID is how we find your town. If you do not have it, write to us anyway. Without it we may not be able to find a guest town.

We reply within 7 days and delete within 30 days. By email we can delete your towns, player ID and sign-in links. We cannot delete play statistics this way: they use a separate random ID that only your phone knows. To delete them, turn Play statistics off in the game, use Delete my account, or install the game again and answer Only on this phone.

Removing Nirmaan Nagar from "Sign in with Apple" in your iPhone's settings does not delete your town. Use the steps above.

Only stop crash reports or play statistics

Tap Settings → Online, then switch Crash reports or Play statistics off.

  • Crash reports off: nothing more is sent, and reports not yet sent are deleted.
  • Play statistics off: nothing more is sent, and Unity is asked to delete what it holds.
